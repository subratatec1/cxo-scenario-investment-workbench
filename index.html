How the leadership score works

Illustrative weighted score: financial value, residual risk reduction, strategic fit and execution feasibility. Finance component = clamp(50 + ROI ÷ 2, 0–100); risk and feasibility = 100 minus their risk scores. Confidence is displayed separately and is not a probability.

Unfunded options are excluded from the suggested shortlist. A score is a discussion aid, not an approval or an AI prediction.

Model methodology and boundaries

Implementation budget including overrun plus transition cost is spread evenly over delivery months. Benefits begin the month after delivery and ramp linearly to full annual run rate over six months. Delays shift expenditure and benefit timing; they do not add cost automatically. Overrun applies only to implementation investment. Discounting uses monthly end-of-period cash flows at the annual discount rate. Payback is undiscounted with linear month interpolation. NPV includes only cash flows inside the selected horizon, with no terminal value.

Incremental baseline is continue-as-is. Existing annual run cost is contextual and is not added to incremental costs. Savings are avoided run costs; no separate avoided-risk benefit is monetised. Tax, inflation, financing, residual asset value, stranded costs beyond the explicit transition allowance and benefit growth are excluded. Validate these before any real investment decision.
